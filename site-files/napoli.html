SSC Napoli

Summary
Football Stories · Season Breakdown

Transfers & Summer PreparationAntonio Conte took charge in Naples with an absolute mandate to rebuild. He reunited with trusted marksman Romelu Lukaku and added Scottish internationals Scott McTominay and Billy Gilmour alongside defensive anchor Alessandro Buongiorno.

Domestic & European CampaignWithout European fixture congestion, Conte’s intense physical drilling propelled Napoli straight to the top of Serie A. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia flourished with tactical freedom, while McTominay emerged as an unstoppable box-crashing presence in a robust midfield trident.

HighlightAntonio Conte transformed Napoli back into a ruthless domestic contender overnight, building a ferocious spine behind Lukaku and McTominay.
